## Overview

Computer Vision R&D project focused on object detection and tracking in complex railway video sequences.

The objective was to combine detection and point tracking to follow relevant objects across frames under challenging real-world conditions.

## My contribution

- Trained and evaluated object-detection models based on YOLOX and YOLOV++.
- Worked with public datasets such as COCO and OVIS for experimentation and model adaptation.
- Fine-tuned models for domain-specific video data.
- Evaluated detection performance at both dataset and individual-video level.
- Used CoTracker3 for point tracking in online and offline configurations.
- Converted detector bounding boxes into tracking queries.
- Experimented with tracking model fine-tuning and evaluation.
- Investigated difficult cases including occlusion, lighting variation, object splitting/merging, and complex motion.
