## Overview

Generative AI R&D project focused on generating temporally coherent video sequences for an automotive application.

The work explored how image-generation architectures could be extended to video by incorporating temporal information and motion-aware components.

## My contribution

- Extended image-generation architectures toward video generation.
- Worked with a video U-Net architecture operating on temporal sequences.
- Integrated and experimented with motion modules.
- Developed temporal data loading and preprocessing workflows.
- Worked with conditional generation, including Canny-based conditioning.
- Developed and tested video inference workflows.
- Evaluated generated outputs using PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS, and FVD.
- Investigated temporal consistency and generation quality across sequences.

## Frameworks and technologies

JoliGEN · DeepDetect · Python · PyTorch · Diffusion Models · Video U-Net · Motion Modules · Temporal Conditioning · OpenCV
