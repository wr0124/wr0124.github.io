## Overview

Generative Computer Vision project exploring synthetic image generation for scenes using image-to-image translation.

The objective was to increase environmental diversity by transforming existing images into different visual conditions while preserving the structure of the original scenes.

## My contribution

- Experimented with CUT and CUT-Turbo for image-to-image translation.
- Used BDD100K images as the experimental dataset.
- Generated daytime-to-night transformations.
- Generated clear-weather to snowy-scene transformations.
- Prepared and organized data for model experimentation.
- Evaluated generated outputs and compared different generation approaches.
- Investigated synthetic-data generation as a method for increasing visual diversity in Computer Vision datasets.
